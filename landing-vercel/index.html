Trust needs proof.

Roma is early. I'm Felix. I'm 20, and this app asks for powerful macOS permissions. Your hesitation is reasonable. Here is the exact deal.

What leaves your Mac

Local transcription keeps audio on your Mac. Choose a cloud model and Roma sends audio to that provider using the credentials you configure.

Cloud polishing can send the transcript, selected text, and any clipboard or screen context you turn on. Recordings stay in local history until you delete them or enable automatic cleanup.

Why Roma asks

Microphone records speech. Input Monitoring detects your chosen shortcut. Accessibility inserts text, reads nearby cursor text for capitalization, and can read selected text for enhancement. Screen Context is optional and reads visible text only when you turn it on.

What is public

The source, releases, and changes are public. The browser demo lets you try the interaction before installing, but it does not test the Mac app. This website loads Vercel Web Analytics. The Mac app has no product-analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Current Mac downloads are not Apple-notarized. Gatekeeper can block the first launch until you choose Open Anyway. If that is too much trust for your main Mac, use the browser demo or wait.
